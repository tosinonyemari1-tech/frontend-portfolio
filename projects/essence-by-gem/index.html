Our Story

Born from a
passion for scent

Essence By Gem was founded with a singular vision: to create fragrances that are more than just scents — they're memories in a bottle. Every perfume in our collection is handcrafted in small batches using the finest natural ingredients sourced from around the world.

We believe that a great fragrance should evolve on your skin throughout the day, revealing different facets and notes as time passes. That's why we compose each scent with top, heart, and base notes that unfold gracefully.
